# Competitive Notes — Storyboard V9

## StudioBinder
Applied principle: keep shot metadata focused on the production essentials. Cast Iraq V9 uses shot size, camera angle and camera movement, with the technical fields hidden behind More Details.

## Boords
Applied principle: frame-first Grid as the primary view plus a secondary Shot List view. Frames can be reordered and edited quickly.

## Milanote
Applied principle: visual simplicity, direct image upload, drag-and-drop organization and notes without building a heavy production-management suite.

## Deliberately NOT copied
AI generation, annotation/drawing canvas, animatics, advanced versioning, client review workflows, call sheets, equipment, wardrobe, locations, script breakdown and complex scheduling.

## Product decision
Cast Iraq's differentiator is not "another storyboard app". The core is:
Photographer finds talent -> adds them to a project -> plans shots around those talents.
